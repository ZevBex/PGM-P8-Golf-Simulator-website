About PGM.

PGM is a company-owned brand operated by 佛山市顺德异邦高尔夫用品有限公司. We combine manufacturing, trading and independent product development for golf equipment, including P8 simulator systems and related components.

From a simulator system to individual components.

The P8 brings together camera-based shot sensing, onboard image processing, simulator software, projection and playing surfaces. The current catalog presents Standard, Flagship and Performance packages, with different projectors, computer descriptions and motion-platform equipment.

Component enquiries can cover automatic ball return, the eight-way motion platform and the mirror touch console. Reviewing each part alongside the complete room helps identify what needs to be supplied, retained or integrated.

Put the important details in writing.

Use the published catalog as the starting point for equipment comparison. Ask for a quotation that identifies the detector, software, projection, computer, screen, playing surfaces and any selected accessories.

Before an order, confirm software rights, delivery and installation responsibilities, component warranty and the support process. Your destination and intended use help define those requirements. Dealer appointment, territory arrangements and any local service responsibilities require agreement for the specific relationship.
