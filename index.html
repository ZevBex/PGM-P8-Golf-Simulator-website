INDEPENDENT PROCESSING. LESS PC WORKLOAD.

Powered by a
Built-in MCU Microcomputer.

The P8 detector processes trajectory image data independently, inside the detector itself. Your computer can focus on simulator software and course graphics.

PC-dependent image processing

In systems that process detector images on the host PC, image processing shares CPU and GPU resources with other tasks. Heavy workloads can contribute to delayed processing or dropped data.

P8 onboard image processing

A built-in MCU handles trajectory image processing independently, reducing reliance on the host PC’s processing resources and helping keep shot feedback responsive and consistent.

  • Independent trajectory image processing
  • Reduced image-processing load on the host PC
  • Designed for responsive, stable shot feedback

How much room do I need?

The reference layouts above are a starting point. A suitable room must also allow safe clearance for your height, swing and longest club. Share the usable depth, width and ceiling height, including beams or other obstructions.

Is the P8 suitable for home and commercial use?

The system can be planned for private golf rooms, clubs and coaching spaces. Tell us your expected usage, number of hitting bays and whether you need both left- and right-handed play.

Does 8K software mean the projector is 8K?

No. PGM describes software output support up to 8K; the laser projector listed on its P8 page is 4K (3840 × 2160). Actual visual output depends on the PC, software settings and display hardware included.

Can I order components without a complete simulator?

Yes. The impact screen, ball return machine, eight-way motion platform, custom mirror-finish console and hitting mat can each be quoted separately. Share your existing equipment and room details so fit and compatibility can be checked.

What is included in my quote?

Ask for an itemised configuration covering the detector, computer, software, projector, screen, console, hitting surface and any platform or entertainment options. Shipping, installation and local taxes should be confirmed separately.

How do installation and after-sales support work?

Availability varies by location and configuration. Include your country and city in your enquiry so installation arrangements, support, warranty terms and software updates can be confirmed before you order.
