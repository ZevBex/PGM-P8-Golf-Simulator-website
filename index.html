PLAN YOUR SETUP

A clearer path to
your first round.

Start with your room, your players and your goals. Here are the key details to work through before choosing a configuration.

How much room do I need?

Measure the clear width, depth and ceiling height of your room. Include doors, beams and other obstructions, and tell us whether left-handed and right-handed golfers will use the bay. The layout needs to account for swing clearance, screen spacing and equipment placement.

What is included in a PGM P8 setup?

Use the component guide above to explore the system. Ask for an itemized quotation that identifies the supplied hardware, screen, hitting surface, projector, computer and optional additions for your selected setup.

Are software and courses included?

Ask for the software edition, included play modes and current course list in your quotation. Confirm any subscription, renewal or upgrade charges before ordering.

What should I check before installation?

Share room photos and dimensions so equipment placement, projector mounting, power and connection requirements can be reviewed for your configuration.

How do I arrange shipping and installation?

Include your destination country, city and postal code with your inquiry. Request shipping terms, estimated lead time, installation scope and any destination charges in the quotation.

What about warranty and support?

Request the written warranty for each supplied component, along with the support process, replacement-part arrangements and any service exclusions before placing an order.
