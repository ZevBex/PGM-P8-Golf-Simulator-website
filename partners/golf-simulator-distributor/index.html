Dealer questions.

How do I enquire about becoming a PGM golf simulator distributor?

Use the form below or contact PGM by email. Include your company website, market, customer types and service capabilities so the team can review the enquiry.

Is a dealership exclusive?

Territory protection and exclusivity are not automatic. Any such arrangement must be discussed and confirmed in a written agreement.

Can I order a demonstration unit?

Ask the team about a suitable demonstration configuration, current availability and applicable order terms. A sample price or free demonstration unit is not offered on this page.

What should my technical team evaluate?

Review room requirements, supplied components, software terms, installation and calibration, remote support and replacement-part procedures. Our room-size guide is a useful starting point.
