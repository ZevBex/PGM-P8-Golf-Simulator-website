Website & enquiries

Privacy and your website choices.

This website is operated by 佛山市顺德异邦高尔夫用品有限公司, owner of the PGM brand. You can contact the team and send an enquiry without accepting optional website analytics.

Information you choose to send.

Enquiry forms ask for your name, email, destination country, optional company details and your project message. Product enquiries also include the selected equipment, enquiry type and source page so the team can understand the request.

These details are submitted to the website’s enquiry service to handle your request and related communication. Please do not include passwords, payment-card data, identity documents or other sensitive information in the message.

For questions about an enquiry record, its retention, or a request to access, correct or delete information, email tracy@pgmgolf.com. Include enough context to identify the relevant enquiry without sending unnecessary personal information.

Optional analytics and fixed channel labels.

Google Analytics loads only after you accept analytics. It measures visits, page views and selected interactions such as contact clicks and confirmed form submissions. Our custom analytics events exclude names, email addresses, company details and messages.

Analytics page URLs omit query strings and fragments. Only predefined channel, product, enquiry-type and source-page labels are used; arbitrary URL parameter values are not sent as those labels. Recognized AI referral labels may be used in the same way as other known marketing channels.

Your analytics choice is saved in this browser for 180 days. Accepted campaign labels are kept in this tab for up to 30 minutes. Choosing Essential only stops analytics in this browser and clears this site’s Analytics cookies and saved campaign labels.

Use Privacy & analytics in the footer to review the choice. Google describes its processing in its Privacy Policy.

Essential delivery, security and media.

Website hosting and delivery, Cloudflare security and Turnstile verification support access and form protection. These services may process connection and technical information needed to provide their functions. Product videos may be delivered from Alibaba’s video CDN when played.

Optional analytics consent does not disable essential delivery or security. If a form cannot be used, you can contact tracy@pgmgolf.com.

Website information reviewed 13 November 2026. Contact the operator for questions about the handling of a particular enquiry.
