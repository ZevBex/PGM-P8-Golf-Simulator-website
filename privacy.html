ENQUIRY PRIVACY

Your enquiry.
Your information.

This notice describes the website’s enquiry form and contact links.

When you send an enquiry

The form asks for your name, email, country or city, intended use and message. You may also include a phone number and room dimensions. These details are used to respond to your product enquiry and prepare a relevant configuration or quotation.

If the form displays an unavailable message, online enquiries are not active and no enquiry is sent.

How your message is delivered

When enabled, the form sends your details to FormSubmit, a third-party form-to-email service, which forwards the enquiry to the configured sales email. Its submission page may use a CAPTCHA. FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for 30 days; consult its current privacy policy for processing details.

Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing emails. Please do not include payment details, identity documents or other sensitive information in your message.

WhatsApp and email links

A WhatsApp link opens WhatsApp with a prefilled product enquiry. It does not send the message automatically. WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply. An email link opens your email application; you decide whether to send the email.

Site storage and hosting

This website does not add analytics, advertising trackers or persistent browser storage. Form entries remain in the page until navigation or submission; your browser may provide its own autofill features. The hosting provider and services you choose to open may process connection information under their own policies.

Questions about your information

Once a sales contact is available, use the contact details shown on the main website or reply to the team’s response to ask about your enquiry information.
