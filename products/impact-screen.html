Double-layer impact screen.

MNQ010 is the double-layer golf simulator impact screen in the current PGM catalog. It combines a projection surface with impact absorption, and is offered with custom sizing for the proposed enclosure and room.

Measure for the screen and its mounting.

Send the proposed image size, enclosure dimensions and mounting structure. Review screen tension, the space behind the screen and surrounding impact protection with the installation team.

Match the projector’s image geometry to the selected screen. The screen dimensions alone do not specify projector throw, player clearance or the room’s safe swing area.

Confirm the supplied screen, fixings and local installation work in the quotation. An impact screen order does not automatically include the detector, projector, computer or enclosure.
