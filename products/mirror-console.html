MNQKZT002 mirror touch console.

The MNQKZT002 all-in-one mirror touch kiosk combines a mirror display, touch operation, a retractable keyboard design and a built-in camera for swing observation. Plan its position and software connection around the way your bay is used.

Plan access to the controls.

Choose the console position as part of the bay layout. Keep player movement, the intended hitting position, cabling and the route used by coaches or guests in mind. Send a room sketch showing where you want to use the touchscreen.

For an existing simulator, provide the detector model, software edition, computer and available connections. Ask which functions are supported by the proposed integration, including the camera’s operation and how swing observation is displayed.

The P8 package comparison lists a mirror touchscreen kiosk as a system component. For a standalone console quote, confirm the display size, computer, accessories and software separately so the supplied scope is clear.

Console planning questions.

Can the console be discussed as a separate component?

Yes. Quote model MNQKZT002 and describe your existing simulator and the functions you want to control. Compatibility and supplied equipment are confirmed for the order.

What does the built-in camera do?

The catalog describes the camera as supporting swing observation. Ask how this function operates with your selected software and display arrangement.

What should I send for a compatibility review?

Send the detector model, simulator software, computer specification, available interfaces and a room plan showing the console position.

Discuss the equipment and your project.

Include the console model, your existing equipment, desired functions and installation destination.
