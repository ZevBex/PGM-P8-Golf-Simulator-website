Plan the room and assembly conditions.

Record clear room dimensions and identify the proposed screen, hitting position, detector and projector. Check access for the frame and screen components, the condition of the floor and nearby obstructions.

The catalog describes fast assembly and a two-hour installation reference. Actual assembly time depends on dimensions, site preparation, crew and supplied scope; it should not be treated as a guaranteed complete installation time for every room.

List the frame, screen, turf, fixings, electronics and local work separately. A pictured detector, projector or ball-return component is not automatically included with an enclosure order.
