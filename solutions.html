Your room.

From a private golf retreat to a busy training bay, begin with the way the room will be used. Explore three directions for your P8 setup.

01 / AT HOME

A round.
On your terms.

Bring regular practice and course play into a dedicated golf room. A single-screen layout is a useful starting point; a curved screen can be explored when you have more space.

Start with
Usable room dimensions and your tallest golfer’s swing.
Consider
P8 tracking, projection, a fitted impact screen and a hitting surface.
Personalize
Touch console, motion platform or a shared entertainment layout.
Plan a home golf room

02 / FOR YOUR VENUE

Make space
for the next group.

Plan a simulator area for golf lounges, gyms, health clubs, members’ clubs and hospitality venues. Consider the number of bays, visitor circulation and seating alongside the playing space.

Start with
Bay count, expected daily usage and player circulation.
Consider
Screen layout, touch controls and ball return, plus safe separation from nearby fitness equipment.
Confirm
Commercial software use, maintenance, support and local installation arrangements.
Discuss a commercial setup

03 / FOR PLAYER DEVELOPMENT

Give practice
a purpose.

Build a teaching space around shot feedback, repeatable practice and clear communication between player and coach. Add slope training or varied hitting surfaces where they support your sessions.

Start with
Teaching format, coach position and left- and right-handed users.
Consider
Vision tracking, a clear projection surface and suitable practice mats.
Develop
Eight-way slope practice and bunker or rough recovery surfaces.
Plan a coaching studio
